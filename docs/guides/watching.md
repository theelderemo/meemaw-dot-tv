# Watching Meemaw.tv

This guide is for viewers, the people the app was set up for. You don't need any technical knowledge. If you're the one *running* Meemaw.tv, you want the [self-hosting guide](self-hosting.md) instead.

Throughout this page, "your operator" means whoever set up Meemaw.tv for your household. They created your account, and they're the person to ask when something's stuck.

## What you need

- The web address of your household's Meemaw.tv. Your operator gives you this.
- The email and password they created for you. There is no sign-up page; accounts are made for you.
- **Chrome or Firefox**, on a computer or a TV that has one of those browsers. Safari and iPhones can't play these streams. That's a limitation of how the video is delivered, not something your operator can fix.

## Signing in

Open the address, type your email and password, and you're in. You only do this once per device; Meemaw.tv keeps you signed in for weeks at a time. If you ever find yourself back at the sign-in page unexpectedly, just sign in again; if it keeps happening, tell your operator.

After signing in you'll see a "Who's watching?" screen with your name on a tile. Click yourself.

## Finding something to watch

The home page works the way you'd expect from any streaming app:

- A big featured title at the top, with Play and More Info buttons.
- Rows of posters below it, grouped by theme. Scroll sideways through a row with the arrows at its edges.
- Point at a poster and a little card pops up with buttons: play, add to your list, more info.
- **More Info** opens the full page for a title: description, cast, and for TV shows, the season and episode list.
- **Movies** and **TV Shows** in the menu show rows for just that kind of thing.
- **Search** (the magnifying glass at the top) finds titles as you type.

## My List

Save any title to your list with the plus button, on the popup card or the More Info page. **My List** in the menu shows everything you've saved. It's yours alone; nobody else in the household sees it.

## Picking up where you left off

Meemaw.tv remembers where you stopped, on its own; there's nothing to save. A **Continue Watching** row on the home page holds anything you're partway through, and pressing Play on a show takes you to the episode you're in the middle of. Stop watching on the TV, and the same spot is waiting on the laptop.

## The player

Press Play and the video fills the screen. Move the mouse to bring the controls up:

- Click the video (or the pause button) to pause and resume.
- Drag the bar along the bottom to jump around; hover over it first to preview the time.
- The speaker icon controls volume.
- On a show, a **next episode** button appears so you don't have to go hunting.
- The back arrow in the corner returns you to browsing.

Starting a video usually takes a few seconds; you'll see the Meemaw loader while it gets ready.

## If the sound or picture is wrong

Occasionally a title starts playing in the wrong language, or the quality disappoints. The player has a **Switch Streams** menu for exactly this: open it, pick a different version from the list, and the video restarts with your choice. Versions marked as recommended are usually the safe bets. Your pick lasts for that sitting.

## When something won't play

Sometimes a title just isn't available right now. That's normal for how Meemaw.tv works, and it's nobody's fault. You'll get a friendly message about what happened, a plain line about what to do, and a small gray code underneath.

Usually the fix is one of: try **Switch Streams**, try a different title, or come back later. If a title never works, or *nothing* works, tell your operator and read them the little gray code; it tells them exactly what went wrong.

## Changing your password

Click your profile picture in the top corner, choose **Change Password**, and set a new one. It changes right there, with no emails or codes.

If you've forgotten your password entirely and can't sign in, your operator can set a new one for you.
